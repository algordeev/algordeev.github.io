Project details

Multi-ASV Trajectory Planning

Coordinated mission planning for several autonomous surface vehicles monitoring cyanobacterial blooms. Multi-objective optimization with NSGA-II and NSGA-III balances collision avoidance, route overlap and workload, with Pareto fronts compared statistically across parallel simulation runs.

Technology

MATLAB · NSGA-II · NSGA-III

Key features

Collision avoidance · Route overlap · Workload balancing · Statistical comparison of Pareto fronts

Project details

Autonomous Tram Digital Twin

An independent project extending my physical autonomous tram prototype into a simulation environment for multi-tram operations. It models passenger demand, stops, traffic signals, track switches and depot dispatch. Scenarios based on tram networks in Nizhny Novgorod and İzmir support experiments with service headways, vehicle dynamics, regenerative braking and flywheel energy storage.

Technology

C++ · TypeScript · WebAssembly · Canvas

Key features

Passenger simulation · Depot dispatch · Headway control · Traffic signals and switches · Energy analysis
